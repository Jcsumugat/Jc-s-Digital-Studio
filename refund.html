Refund Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Jc's Digital Studio sells digital products (printable planners and templates). Because these are downloadable files that cannot be returned, refunds are handled as described below. I want every customer to end up with a file that works.

1. 7-day guarantee

You may request a refund within 7 days of purchase if any of the following applies:

  • The file will not open, is corrupted, or cannot be downloaded.
  • The product is materially different from its description on the website.
  • You were charged more than once for the same product.

In the first two cases I will first try to fix the problem or send a working file. If I cannot, you will get a full refund.

2. What is not refundable

  • Change of mind after the file has been downloaded.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after purchase.
  • Problems caused by software or devices that cannot open standard PDF or Word files.

3. How to request a refund

Email jcsumugatxd@gmail.com within the 7-day window and include:

  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • Your order number or receipt (from the Lemon Squeezy confirmation email).
  • A short description of the problem.

I reply within 48 hours.

4. How refunds are paid

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it can take several business days for the money to appear. Currency conversion differences or fees applied by your bank are outside my control.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email jcsumugatxd@gmail.com.
