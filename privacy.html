Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Jc's Digital Studio ("I", "me", "my") collects, why, and what your rights are. I am John Carlo Sumugat, based in the Philippines, and I act as the personal information controller for the information described below. I handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information I collect

When you buy a product. Checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects the details needed to process your order, such as your name, email address, billing country or address, and payment information. Your full card or payment details go to Lemon Squeezy and its payment providers; I do not receive or store them. I receive order information from Lemon Squeezy, such as your name, email address, country, the product purchased, and order details.

When you email me. If you contact me for support or a refund, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

When you visit the website. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser information to deliver the site, under its own privacy statement. I do not run advertising trackers on this site, and I do not use cookies for tracking. If I add analytics in the future, I will update this policy.

2. How I use your information

  • To deliver your purchase and confirm your order.
  • To provide support, fix problems, and process refunds.
  • To keep records required for accounting, tax, and legal compliance.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of the products.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for marketing unless you separately and clearly agree to receive it.

3. Who I share it with

I only share information where needed to run the store:

  • Lemon Squeezy, for checkout, payments, taxes, delivery, and refunds.
  • Email and hosting providers, such as Gmail and GitHub, which I use to communicate with you and host this site.
  • Authorities, if I am required to do so by law.

These providers may process data outside the Philippines. They handle it under their own privacy policies.

4. How long I keep it

I keep order and support records only as long as needed for the purposes above, including any legal, tax, or accounting requirements. After that, I delete or anonymize them.

5. How I protect it

I use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect your information, including limiting access to my own accounts and using secure, reputable service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I take care with the data entrusted to me.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed.
  • Access the personal data I hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Object to processing, or withdraw your consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request erasure or blocking of your data, subject to records I must keep by law.
  • Data portability, where applicable.
  • Claim damages if your data is misused.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, email me at jcsumugatxd@gmail.com. I respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first.

7. Children

This store is not directed at children. If you are under the age required to enter a contract where you live, please ask a parent or guardian to make the purchase.

8. External links

This site may link to other websites, such as Lemon Squeezy's checkout. I am not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
